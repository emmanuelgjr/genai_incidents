Upstream terms (REUSE identifier: LicenseRef-upstream-terms)

This is NOT a licence grant and it is not the text of any licence. It is a
placeholder identifier, used in .reuse/dep5, that means:

    The files so marked are third-party upstream snapshots or hand-curated
    records derived from third-party sources. They remain under each
    upstream source's OWN terms, which differ from source to source. They are
    not covered by this repository's CC BY 4.0 data grant (LICENSE-DATA) or by
    its MIT code licence (LICENSE), and nothing in this repository grants you
    rights in them beyond what the upstream grants.

Where to find the terms for a given file:

  - docs/SOURCE_LICENSES.md has one row per upstream source: its licence (or
    the fact that none was found), what is permitted, what this project does
    with it, the operative clause quoted with its URL, and the date checked.
  - NOTICE-DATA records the specific attributions and notices the upstreams
    require to accompany copies (for example CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution, the
    CVE Terms of Use notice, the MIT notice for AVID, and the Apache License
    2.0 texts for MITRE ATLAS and garak).
  - ingest/README.md maps each file under ingest/ to its source.

Some files marked with this identifier contain wording written by this
project's maintainers (short summaries and links) on top of third-party facts.
That does not change the marking: the file as a whole is distributed under the
upstream terms described above, and no specific licence (CC0, CC BY, or any
other) is claimed for it by this identifier.

If an upstream's terms are unknown, docs/SOURCE_LICENSES.md says UNKNOWN for
that source; UNKNOWN means no rights are claimed, not that rights are granted.
